Knowledge & Treatment Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our dental procedures, clinic schedule in Sevilla, 5:00 PM cut-off, installment plans, and sterile clinical standards.

1. Appointments & Clinic Hours

Our official operating hours are:

  • Monday to Saturday: 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM
  • Sunday: 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM

Please take note that our daily cut-off for both pre-booked patients and walk-ins is strictly 5:00 PM to ensure adequate time for comprehensive clinical procedures and multi-stage operatory disinfection.

We warmly accommodate walk-in patients whenever operatory chairs are available. However, to guarantee zero waiting time and ensure you can consult with your preferred doctor (Dr. Micah Redilla or Dr. Pauline Trinidad), we strongly advise booking via our website or texting us ahead at (0976) 485 7648.

Initial comprehensive oral examination and diagnosis ranges between ₱500 to ₱800. For patients who proceed immediately with their proposed treatment (such as tooth restoration, cleaning, or orthodontic downpayment), this fee is credited toward your procedure.

2. Treatments, Braces & Child Care

During your diagnostic assessment, Dr. Micah Redilla conducts a photographic evaluation, tooth bite analysis, and 3D typodont study. Traditional metal braces provide optimal torque for severe crowding and bite correction, while ceramic brackets offer a subtle tooth-colored aesthetic. We'll present the advantages of each option so you can decide comfortably.

Bracket placement is completely painless and non-invasive. You may feel mild tenderness or pressure for 24 to 48 hours following monthly wire adjustments as your teeth begin shifting into their ideal alignment. We provide complimentary orthodontic relief wax and guidance on soft foods.

We specialize in positive reinforcement and child psychology. We hand each young patient a pair of playful green dinosaur sunglasses ("Dino Shades") to shield their eyes from the dental examination light. We explain instruments as playful space tools, use topical non-stinging numbing gels, and award bravery stickers so children look forward to future checkups!

Teeth whitening lightens your natural tooth enamel by breaking down coffee, tea, or aging stains using laser light activation. Direct composite veneers, hand-sculpted by Dr. Pauline Trinidad (CODAC Smile Architect), cover the entire front face of teeth to permanently close gaps, alter tooth shapes, and mask severe intrinsic discoloration in a single appointment.

3. Pricing, Payments & Installments

Yes! We offer budget-friendly orthodontic installment arrangements. You can begin with a minimal initial downpayment (covering diagnostic models, brackets, and initial bonding), followed by affordable monthly adjustment fees (₱1,000–₱1,500) until treatment completion.

We accept Cash, GCash, Maya, direct online bank transfers (BDO, BPI, UnionBank), and major Visa and MasterCard credit cards.

We provide itemized Official Receipts (OR) and signed HMO claim forms for hassle-free reimbursement with your corporate or private dental insurance provider.

  • Ground Floor tenant: Technoblitz Computer Store

Every instrument undergoes ultrasonic cleaning, chemical bath immersion, and medical-grade Class B Vacuum Autoclave sterilization. Instruments are individually sealed in indicator barrier pouches and opened chairside exclusively in front of the patient.
