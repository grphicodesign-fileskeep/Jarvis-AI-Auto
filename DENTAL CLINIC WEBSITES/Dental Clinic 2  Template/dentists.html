Patient Safety Protocol

Hospital-Grade Autoclave Sterilization in Every Operatory

At DentaCure, infection prevention is uncompromising. All surgical and restorative handpieces, burs, and dental instruments undergo strict multi-stage decontamination:

Class B Medical Vacuum Autoclave

Pressurized steam heat cycle eliminating 100% of bacterial and viral pathogens.

Individual Sealed Pouch Openings

Every instrument pack remains sealed with biological indicator strips until opened chairside in your presence.

Surface Barrier Disinfection

Dental operatory chairs, suction lines, and light handles are chemically sanitized and sleeved between every patient visit.
