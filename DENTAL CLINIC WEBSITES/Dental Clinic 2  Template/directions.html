How to Reach DentaCure Sevilla

Detailed driving directions and public commute guidelines from anywhere in La Union.

By Car or Motorcycle

Drive along McArthur Highway into Brgy. Sevilla. Look for Grand Plaza Building located along the main road. The ground floor displays prominent signage for Technoblitz Computer Store.

🅿️ Dedicated Parking: Convenient street-level parking bays available right in front of Grand Plaza Building for all dental patients.

By Jeepney or Tricycle

From San Fernando City Plaza or Market, board any southbound jeepney (bound for Bauang, Caba, or Aringay) and ask the driver to drop you at Grand Plaza Building in Sevilla, across ILAW ATBP.

🚏 Landmark Drop-off: Tell the driver or tricycle operator: "Grand Plaza Building Sevilla tapat ng ILAW ATBP. / taas ng Technoblitz."

Directions & Parking FAQ

Quick answers to common arrival and visit questions.

1 Where is parking located at Grand Plaza Building?

Complimentary street-level parking spaces are available directly in front of Grand Plaza Building for all DentaCure patients. Dedicated motorcycle parking bays are also provided.

2 How do I access Unit 1 on the 2nd Floor?

Enter the main entrance of Grand Plaza Building beside Technoblitz Computer Store and take the stairs to the 2nd Floor. Unit 1 is the first clinic suite with clear DentaCure glass signage.

3 Do you accept walk-in patients or are appointments required?

We welcome walk-in patients during clinic hours, though our daily appointment and walk-in cut-off is strictly at 5:00 PM. To avoid waiting and secure your preferred doctor (Dr. Micah Redilla or Dr. Pauline Trinidad), booking online or texting us at (0976) 485 7648 is recommended.
